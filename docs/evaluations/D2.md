### Finding F1 — segmented RSVP button label wraps

- **Where:** `event-detail.html`, "Your RSVP" segmented control. Visible in `./docs/mocks/screenshots/event-detail.1440.png` (pre-fix).
- **What:** The third segment, "Can't go", broke to two lines while the other two segments fit on one. Cause: `.segmented` is `display: inline-flex` with no width constraint and `.segmented__item { flex: 1 }`; the equal-flex-share width was occasionally narrower than the content of the widest item, so the longest label wrapped.
- **Severity:** Visual polish. Blocks criterion 4 (Great UX) since the wrap was inconsistent and looked broken.
- **Fix:** added `white-space: nowrap` to `.segmented__item` and bumped horizontal padding from `var(--space-3)` (12px) to `var(--space-4)` (16px) so the equal-flex shares are comfortably wide enough for "Can't go".
- **Verification:** re-ran `node ./docs/mocks/render.mjs`. New `event-detail.360.png` and `event-detail.1440.png` show all three segments on one line each, equally spaced.
