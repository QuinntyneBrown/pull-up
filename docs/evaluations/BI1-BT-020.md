## Behavior

Command implements both `IRequest<Unit>` and `IAuthorizationRequirement`. The colocated `UpdateEventAuthorizationHandler` enforces host-only (passing auth on missing-event so the handler can 404). The handler snapshots `StartsAtUtc`, `EndsAtUtc`, and `Location` **before** calling `event.UpdateDetails(...)`, then after `SaveChangesAsync` compares to decide whether to fan out `EventUpdated` notifications. Description-only edits skip the dispatch entirely.
